# Demo SOP — not an approved field procedure.
## sop-recover-01 Recovery validation
### sop-recover-01#1 Confirm recovery
Recovery is confirmed only after voltage, inflow and the reserve horizon are all healthy for five consecutive steps. An approved action is not a recovery.
### sop-recover-01#2 Close out
Record the resolution step, the actions taken and the remaining limitations, then export the incident report.
