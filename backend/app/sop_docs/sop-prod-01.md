# Demo SOP — not an approved field procedure.
## sop-prod-01 Reduced water production
### sop-prod-01#1 Compare inflow with plant power
Confirm that reduced inflow coincides with reduced production power or a voltage dip. Do not assume a pump fault without a second indicator.
### sop-prod-01#2 Check plant state
Ask the water operator to check the plant controller status and pump alarms. A drop in production that follows a voltage dip is expected to recover after the feed is restored.
### sop-prod-01#3 Record
Log inflow before and after the drop and the time the plant output stabilised.
