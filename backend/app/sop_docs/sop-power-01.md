# Demo SOP — not an approved field procedure.
## sop-power-01 Power instability inspection
### sop-power-01#1 Confirm the reading
Compare supply voltage against the nominal 230 V band on the next two readings. Treat a single low reading as noise; a sustained dip below 210 V needs inspection.
### sop-power-01#2 Inspect the feed
Check the distribution feed and breaker status for the pumping station. If the feed is degraded, request restoration of the power feed (simulated command restore_power_feed in this demo).
### sop-power-01#3 Record
Log the voltage range, the first step below 210 V and who was notified.
