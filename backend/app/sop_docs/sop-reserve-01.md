# Demo SOP — not an approved field procedure.
## sop-reserve-01 Reserve conservation
### sop-reserve-01#1 Check the horizon
When the calculated hours to critical reserve fall below 16 h with outflow exceeding inflow, prepare demand conservation. The forecast assumes rates stay constant.
### sop-reserve-01#2 Conserve demand
Request voluntary demand conservation and prioritise essential users (simulated command conserve_demand in this demo). Re-check the horizon after the next window.
### sop-reserve-01#3 Escalate
If the reserve is projected to reach the critical level within 6 hours, escalate to the resilience officer for an alternative supply decision.
